PETRA, JORDAN

Nestled in the rugged mountains of southern Jordan, the ancient city of Petra stands as a remarkable testament to the ingenuity and artistry of the Nabataeans.
Carved directly into the red sandstone cliffs, Petra's most iconic structure is the Treasury (Al-Khazneh), a magnificent façade that has captivated visitors for centuries.
The city served as a crucial trading hub along the incense route, connecting Arabia, Egypt, and the Mediterranean world.
Petra's architectural marvels include elaborate tombs, a royal palace, and an impressive amphitheater carved into the mountainside.
Despite being abandoned for several centuries, Petra was rediscovered by Swiss explorer Johann Ludwig Burckhardt in 1812 and has since become one of the most visited archaeological sites in the world.
