GREAT PYRAMID OF GIZA

In the sands of Egypt, the ancient watch of the Great Pyramid of Giza still stares down time itself.
Built as a tomb for the Pharaoh Khufu around 2560 BC, the Great Pyramid of Giza is the oldest and only surviving wonder of the original Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
Rising to an original height of 146.6 meters (481 feet), it was the tallest man-made structure in the world for over 3,800 years.
Constructed from an estimated 2.3 million blocks of limestone and granite, the pyramid's precise alignment with the cardinal points and its complex internal chambers continue to fascinate archaeologists and historians.
The Great Pyramid stands as a testament to the ingenuity and skill of ancient Egyptian civilization, symbolizing their beliefs in the afterlife and their reverence for their pharaohs.
Today, it remains a powerful symbol of human achievement and a source of wonder for visitors from around the world.
