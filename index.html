WONDERS OF THE WORLD

A dive into the wonders of the world.
Eight wonders rise from the bones of the earth,
eight defiant monuments whispering the same truth:
humans are small… but our dreams are enormous.
Stone was stacked against gravity.
Mountains were carved like clay.
Jungles were conquered, deserts endured,
marble shaped into grief, faith, and glory.
In the sands of Egypt, the ancient watch of the Great Pyramid of Giza still stares down time itself.
Across the ridges of China, the endless spine of the Great Wall of China crawls across mountains like a sleeping dragon.
Hidden in rose-red stone stands Petra, a city carved from silence.
High above Brazil, Christ the Redeemer stretches open arms over a restless world.
Above the clouds of the Andes, Machu Picchu clings to the mountains like a secret.
In the heat of Mexico, the steps of Chichen Itza climb toward the sky.
In Rome, the scarred stone of the Colosseum remembers the roar of ancient crowds.
And in India, the white marble heart of the Taj Mahal mourns a love that refused to fade.
Humanity was here.
Humanity dared.
Humanity built the impossible.
