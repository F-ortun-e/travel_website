TAJ MAHAL, INDIA

Located in Agra, India, the Taj Mahal is a magnificent mausoleum built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal.
Constructed between 1632 and 1648, the Taj Mahal is renowned for its symmetrical design and the use of white marble adorned with intricate inlay work.
The structure is a masterpiece of Mughal architecture and is considered one of the finest examples of Islamic architecture in the world.
The Taj Mahal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and attracts millions of visitors from around the world who come to admire its beauty and learn about its rich history.
