CHICHEN ITZA, MEXICO

Nestled in the Yucatan Peninsula of Mexico, the ancient city of Chichen Itza stands as a remarkable testament to the ingenuity and artistry of the Maya civilization.
Built during the Late Classic and Early Postclassic periods (c. 600–1200 AD), Chichen Itza was a major center of trade and culture.
The site is renowned for its impressive architecture, including the iconic El Castillo pyramid and the Great Ball Court.
Chichen Itza is considered one of the most important archaeological sites in Mesoamerica and a symbol of Maya civilization.
Today, it attracts millions of visitors from around the world who come to admire its beauty and learn about its rich history.
